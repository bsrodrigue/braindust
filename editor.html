Controls

Drag with a tool to draw or place. Right/middle drag or Space + drag pans, wheel zooms.
Corners of a selected wall resize it. Arrows nudge (Shift ×10). Del removes, Ctrl+D duplicates.
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo / redo, Ctrl+S saves, G toggles the 10 px grid snap, F frames the level.
The seafloor and its 3D graveyard sit at the level's depth; keep a floor wall there. 10 px = 1 m.
